03 — How dual@TUHH actually works

One employer, one degree, six semesters

Every TUHH bachelor's can be studied dually. You sign an employment contract with a partner company before your first semester and stay with them — on payroll — for the whole degree.

Pillar 01

University study

The exact same lectures, exams and Bachelor thesis as full‑time students — no separate "dual curriculum" academically.

Pillar 02

Practice phases

Five Praxismodule during lecture‑free periods at your partner company, worth 30 of the total 210 ECTS.

Pillar 03

Theory‑practice transfer

Dedicated soft‑skill and personal‑development seminars (TPV) replace the standard non‑technical electives.

08 — Important details

What else you need to know

🎓

Admission requirements

You need Allgemeine Hochschulreife (general university entrance qualification). Fachhochschulreife is not accepted. For foreign diplomas, check equivalence at anabin.kmk.org. There is no NC (Numerus Clausus) for any of the three programs.

🌐

Language requirements

  • Computer Science: German DSH‑2 / TestDaF 16 / telc C1 Hochschule (English not formally required but essential)
  • Data Science: German DSH‑2 + English expected (mixed teaching)
  • Informatik‑Ingenieurwesen: German DSH‑2 / TestDaF 16 / telc C1 Hochschule

Check exact requirements on the TUHH language page.

📚

Grundpraktikum (pre-study internship)

IIW requires an 8-week technical internship (Grundpraktikum) — ideally done before starting. CS and DS do not require one.

Dual students are exempt — the Vorpraktikum at your company replaces it.

💶

Costs & Semesterbeitrag

There is no tuition at TUHH. You pay a Semesterbeitrag of ~€350/semester (includes public transport ticket). Some dual partner companies reimburse this — check your Studierendenvertrag. Dual students receive ~€1,100+/month salary year-round.

🔀

Switching: regular ↔ dual

You can switch from regular to dual before the end of semester 2 — just find a company and sign a Studierendenvertrag. You can also switch from dual to regular at any time. Already enrolled students can look for "sofort" tagged companies in the database.

⬆️

Master pathways

Each bachelor leads to several master options. As a TUHH graduate, you get guaranteed admission to consecutive, non-restricted masters:

  • IIW → IIW, CS, Data Science, Info & Comm Systems, Elektrotechnik
  • CS → CS, and others with elective prep
  • DS → DS, CS, and others with elective prep
